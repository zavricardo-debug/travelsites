Off-price playbook: unlike outlets, these stores sell regular-season surplus (no factory seconds), and stock rotates constantly — visit early in the week for the freshest drops. Returns are easy (30–90 days). And remember: no sales tax on clothing in D.C., so an off-price trip in the city can beat a trek to the suburbs.
Tax tip: D.C. has no sales tax on clothing — a real saving vs. most U.S. cities. Virginia adds a small tax but has cheaper groceries/restaurants; Maryland sometimes runs back-to-school tax holidays. And CityCenterDC & Georgetown are safest bets for tourist-friendly hours (open Sundays).

07 — Plan your time

Suggested Itineraries

Day 1 Classic Monuments Loop

  1. White House + Lafayette Square (outside view, 30 min)
  2. Washington Monument — elevator to the top (book ticket)
  3. Walk the Mall east: National Gallery of Art (1.5 h)
  4. U.S. Capitol — free tour + visitor galleries
  5. Sunset at the Lincoln Memorial, then the Vietnam & Korean War Memorials (they glow at night)

Day 2 Museums + Tidal Basin

  1. Air & Space Museum (timed pass) — 2–3 h
  2. American History or Natural History for lunch + afternoon
  3. Tidal Basin loop: Jefferson, MLK, FDR Memorials
  4. Dinner cheap in Georgetown (Falafel Inc.) + an evening stroll on M Street

Day 3 Shop & Outlet Day

  1. Morning: Eastern Market on Capitol Hill (weekend best)
  2. Lunch: Union Market + local makers
  3. Afternoon: Tanger Outlets National Harbor or Leesburg Premium Outlets (car)
  4. Evening: Georgetown or The Wharf waterfront + $$ dinner

09 — Know before you go

Practical Tips

1Get around by Metro. Fares ≈ $2–6, day trips by SmarTrip/contactless card. Hotels in the center + Metro = no rental car needed. Bus + Metro reaches National Harbor and Tysons.
2Book free passes early. Air & Space, Library of Congress and the Washington Monument all need free timed tickets — 2–6 weeks ahead in high season (Mar–Jun, Sep–Oct).
3Best seasons: late March–April (cherry blossoms, busy!), Sept–Oct (cool + golden light), Dec (holiday decorations). July–Aug is hot and humid — go early and hydrate.
4See monuments twice. They're open 24h and free — at night with the lights on they're arguably better, and you'll avoid the crowds. Watch your step after dark.
5Tipping (15–20%) is expected at sit-down restaurants. Counter-service spots: no tip needed. Tap water is free everywhere.
6Safety. The Mall and tourist core are safe and well-policed, day and night. Use normal city sense on Metro late evening, and keep valuables close in crowds.
7Plan around the markets. Sat: Eastern Market flea (09:00–16:00) + Mt. Pleasant (morning). Sun: Georgetown Flea (08:00–16:00) + Dupont FRESHFARM (until 13:30). Fri: USDA market right off the Mall (May–Oct). Thu: Penn Quarter (Apr–Nov). Dec: DC Holiday Market (Nov 12 – Dec 23, 2026) at Dupont Circle.
