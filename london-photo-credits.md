# London guide — photo sources & licensing

`index.html` is fully self-contained: every photograph below is embedded as a base64 JPEG
data URI (13 images, ~1.1 MB of the 1.4 MB file). They were resized/cropped to 4:3
(cards) and 1222×660 (hero) and JPEG-compressed for web.

**These are real photographs gathered from public web pages via image search, not
licensed stock.** Before publishing commercially or reselling the page, either confirm
the licence with each source, replace them with your own shots, or swap in licensed stock.
Each file name shows where the image is used.

## Note on the content

Prices, opening hours and market days were researched in **September–October 2026** from
official sites (museum and attraction pages, TfL fares pages, market operators) plus
reputable travel guides, and are approximate. Fares (bus £1.75, Zones 1–2 daily cap
£8.90) and free museum admission are stable; special exhibitions, market hours and
ticketed landmark prices change often — the page tells readers to confirm before travelling.
